## HP2 result

The PressClub tag library, BMW Motorrad UK manuals entry point, and the relevant BMW Motorrad UK historic range landing page were checked for each HP2 model. They did not expose a clearly identifiable technical document for the HP2 Megamoto or HP2 Sport. Their catalogue identities remain exact from their explicit titles; technical fields remain unresolved with those three checked URLs stored at field level in the JSON. This is intentional: no figures have been backfilled from non-official sources.

The complete per-record, per-field evidence—including URL, document/page label, market, applicable years, review date, confidence, and checked-source lists—is in `motorrad-derivative-catalogue.json` under `technicalEvidence`.
