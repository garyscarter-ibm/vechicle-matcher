## Editorial decisions still requiring review

- Category, fuel classification, size band and character tags are explicit editorial comparisons for every record; they are not BMW technical claims.
- Acceleration, seating and standard luggage remain unavailable for every record lacking existing official evidence.
- Optional panniers/top boxes are excluded from proposed standard luggage.
- Electric derivatives retain no petrol-mpg claim; their existing range requires separate official evidence before derivative-specific use.

## Recommended staged cutover

1. Review and approve the editorial category/size/tag taxonomy with product stakeholders; it is currently shadow-only.
2. Enable only sourced mass and calculated power-to-weight changes, with A1/A2 regression checks.
3. Review official consumption coverage and then enable calculated UK-mpg values for the small sourced subset.
4. Keep performance, seating and luggage derivative claims disabled until source evidence or an explicit policy is approved.
5. Re-run this shadow audit and compare live stock before each scoped production enablement.
